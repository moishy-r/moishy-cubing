moishy-cubing

A TypeScript toolkit for Rubik's cube speedsolving-method solvers, and the tools built on it.

APB solverdemo

Paste a scramble, get the solve a human following the APB method would actually execute — every step labelled, ranked by how ergonomic it is to turn. Runs entirely in your browser.

CROPtrainer

Random Orientation Prompt. A timer that tells you which orientation to hold the cube in for your next solve — all 24, x2y, or your own set. Also a Chrome extension that does the same inside csTimer.

Packages on JSR and npm: @moishy/cubing-core, @moishy/algsets, @moishy/apb.

Source and documentation on GitHub.
